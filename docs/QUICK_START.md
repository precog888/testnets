# Quick Start Guide

## Welcome!

This guide will help you start working with this repository in a controlled, comfortable way.

## First Steps

### 1. Understand the Purpose

This repository is for:
- Testing AI agent workflows
- Iterative development with clear control
- Learning and documenting best practices

### 2. Choose Your Pace

Pick what works for you:

**🐢 Extra Careful Mode**:
- See every change before it's made
- Approve each small step
- Lots of explanation

**🚶 Step-by-Step Mode** (Recommended):
- One change at a time
- Brief explanations
- Approve as you go

**🏃 Trust Mode**:
- Approve multiple related changes
- Less back-and-forth
- Review after implementation

### 3. Read the Guidelines

Quick reads:
- [README.md](../README.md) - 2 minutes
- [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) - 5 minutes

Deep dive (optional):
- [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) - 10 minutes
- [RECURSIVE_AGENT.md](RECURSIVE_AGENT.md) - 15 minutes

## Example First Session

### Starting a Task

**You say:**
"I want to add a feature to RecursiveAgent. Let's go step by step."

**AI should respond with:**
1. Confirmation of step-by-step approach
2. Questions to understand the feature
3. Wait for your answers

**You respond:**
Answer questions, add any constraints

**AI proposes:**
One specific first step (smallest possible)

**You decide:**
- ✅ "Yes, do it"
- 👀 "Show me the code first"
- 🔄 "Different approach: ..."
- ❌ "No, let's do X instead"

### During Implementation

After each step:
1. AI commits the change
2. Shows what changed
3. Waits for verification
4. You test/review
5. Confirm before next step

### Keeping Control

At any point, you can:
- "Stop"
- "Wait"  
- "Show me"
- "Explain"
- "Different approach"
- "Let me think"

## Common Patterns

### When You Want More Control

Say:
- "Smaller steps please"
- "Show me every change before making it"
- "Explain more"
- "Wait for my approval"

### When Process is Too Slow

Say:
- "You can combine these steps"
- "Less explanation needed"
- "Go ahead with related changes"
- "I trust you on this"

### When Something's Wrong

Say:
- "Stop, that's not what I wanted"
- "Go back"
- "Undo that change"
- "Different approach"

## Useful Commands

### To AI

**Pace Control**:
- "Slow down"
- "Speed up"
- "One at a time"

**Visibility**:
- "Show me the code"
- "Explain why"
- "What are the alternatives?"

**Direction**:
- "Different approach"
- "Let's try..."
- "Focus on..."

**Quality Control**:
- "Test this first"
- "Review before proceeding"
- "Is this safe?"

## Getting Help

### Something's Not Working

1. Use the [feedback template](../templates/FEEDBACK_TEMPLATE.md)
2. Be specific about the issue
3. Suggest what would work better
4. The workflow adapts to you

### Want Different Approach

It's okay to:
- Change your mind
- Try different tools
- Mix approaches
- Take breaks
- Start over

### Comparing with Other Tools

If something works better elsewhere:
- Tell us what works
- Explain why it's better
- We'll adapt

## Tips for Success

### DO:
✅ Speak up when it's too fast
✅ Ask questions
✅ Request explanations
✅ Take your time
✅ Pause when needed
✅ Change the process if it's not working

### DON'T:
❌ Feel pressured to approve things
❌ Accept changes you don't understand
❌ Let process go faster than comfortable
❌ Ignore your preferences
❌ Stick with approach that's not working

## Next Steps

**You're in control.**

The AI is here to assist at YOUR pace, YOUR way.

If something's not working, say so.

The workflow changes to fit you, not the other way around.

## Questions?

Common questions:

**Q: What if I make a mistake approving something?**
A: We can undo it. Git makes this easy.

**Q: What if AI does something I didn't approve?**
A: Tell it immediately. That's a violation of the process.

**Q: Can I change my preferred pace mid-task?**
A: Absolutely. Just say "let's slow down" or "you can move faster".

**Q: What if I want to use a different tool for part of the work?**
A: That's fine. Use whatever works best for each part.

**Q: Is it okay to take breaks between steps?**
A: Yes! Work at your own pace. The state is saved.
